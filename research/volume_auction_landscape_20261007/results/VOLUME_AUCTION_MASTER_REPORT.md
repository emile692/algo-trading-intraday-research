3. **Does low price impact per unit volume predict reversal, breakout, or nothing?** No universal outcome. Most representatives imply nothing after costs; MNQ's high-volume/low-response reversal proxy is a cost-robust watchlist effect, while MGC's breakout-like proxy fails the +2-tick stress.
4. **Do VAH/VAL/POC structures contain reproducible alpha?** No profile representative survives the full profile-specific gate. 5. **Is true volume-at-price required?** Yes for defensible LVN/HVN and order-flow/absorption claims. OHLCV profiles are sufficient only for preliminary rejection/watchlist screening.
6. **Which mechanisms replicate cross-asset?** The mechanism map above reports positive and robust asset counts; correlated index contracts are discounted using the dependence file.
7. **Which effects survive realistic costs?** M2K/price_move_without_volume_confirmation, MES/price_move_without_volume_confirmation, MGC/volume_climax_reversal, MGC/price_move_without_volume_confirmation, MNQ/price_volume_impact_efficiency, MNQ/high_volume_low_response_proxy, MNQ/price_move_without_volume_confirmation, MNQ/volume_shock_after_quiet, MNQ/poc_rejection, NQ/price_move_without_volume_confirmation, NQ/volume_shock_after_quiet, NQ/poc_rejection survived the stringent +2 ticks/side representative stress; see all scenarios in the cost file.

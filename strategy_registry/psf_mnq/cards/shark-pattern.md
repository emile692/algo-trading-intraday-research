## Description publiée

Modern harmonic using 113% reciprocal ratio for aggressive reversal entries.

- **Entrée annoncée** : Enter when C extends to 113% of XA with momentum divergence. Price should show rejection at the 88.6%-113% zone.
- **Sortie annoncée** : Target 50% retracement of BC (the 5-0 completion). Stop beyond 127% XA extension.

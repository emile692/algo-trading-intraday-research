## Description publiée

Trades the classic NY session reversal pattern at 10:30 AM EST.

- **Entrée annoncée** : Identify morning direction. Enter counter-trend at 10:00-10:30 EST with market structure shift confirmation.
- **Sortie annoncée** : Target the PM session high/low or the opening print. Stop beyond the morning extreme.

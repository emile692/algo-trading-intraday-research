## Description publiée

Identifies flag continuation patterns — sharp move followed by a tight counter-trend channel.

- **Entrée annoncée** : Enter long on break above bull flag upper boundary. Short on break below bear flag lower boundary. Flag should retrace < 38.2% of pole.
- **Sortie annoncée** : Target: flagpole length projected from breakout. Stop at flag's extreme opposite end. Trail at flag boundary.

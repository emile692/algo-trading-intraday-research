## Description publiée

Extended harmonic pattern with D point beyond X for deep reversal entries.

- **Entrée annoncée** : Enter at point D (127.2%-161.8% extension of XA) with RSI divergence confirmation. Look for pin bar or engulfing at PRZ.
- **Sortie annoncée** : Target 61.8% and 100% retracement of CD. Stop beyond 161.8% XA extension. Tight initial risk.

## Description publiée

Classic harmonic pattern with precise Fibonacci ratios for reversal entries.

- **Entrée annoncée** : Enter at point D (78.6% retracement of XA) when price reaches the PRZ with confirmation candle. AB=CD completion adds confluence.
- **Sortie annoncée** : Target 38.2% and 61.8% of AD leg. Stop below X point. Scale out at each target level.

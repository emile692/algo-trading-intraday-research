## Description publiée

Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution — the three phases of every trading session.

- **Entrée annoncée** : Identify the Asian range. During London open, wait for a sweep of the Asian high/low (manipulation). Enter on reversal with displacement candle in the direction of the higher-timeframe bias.
- **Sortie annoncée** : Target the opposing side of the Asian range or higher-timeframe liquidity. Stop beyond the manipulation swing.

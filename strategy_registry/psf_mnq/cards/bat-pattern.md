## Description publiée

Harmonic pattern with tight PRZ at 88.6% retracement for precise entries.

- **Entrée annoncée** : Enter at point D (88.6% retracement of XA) with bullish/bearish confirmation candle. B must retrace 38.2%-50% of XA.
- **Sortie annoncée** : Target 38.2% of AD then 61.8% of AD. Stop beyond X. Risk-reward typically 3:1 or better.

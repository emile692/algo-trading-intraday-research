## Description publiée

Smart money accumulation pattern: sweep lows, accumulate, then markup.

- **Entrée annoncée** : Enter long after sell-side liquidity sweep + bullish market structure shift + FVG fill.
- **Sortie annoncée** : Target buy-side liquidity above the range. Stop below the liquidity sweep low.

- **Indicateurs cités** : Liquidity Levels, Market Structure, FVG

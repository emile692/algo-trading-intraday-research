## Description publiée

Nicolas Darvas' box theory for capturing strong breakout moves.

- **Entrée annoncée** : Identify a Darvas Box (new high followed by 3 bars of consolidation establishing a floor). Enter long on break above box top with volume surge.
- **Sortie annoncée** : Stop at the box bottom. Trail stop by building new boxes as the trend continues. Exit on a break below any box bottom.

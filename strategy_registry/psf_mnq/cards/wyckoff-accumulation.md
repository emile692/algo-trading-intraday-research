## Description publiée

Richard Wyckoff's accumulation/distribution schematic for institutional trade tracking.

- **Entrée annoncée** : Identify a trading range with declining volume. Enter long on the 'Spring' — a brief break below range support with volume spike followed by immediate recovery above support.
- **Sortie annoncée** : Target the top of the accumulation range, then the Sign of Strength breakout. Stop below the Spring low.
